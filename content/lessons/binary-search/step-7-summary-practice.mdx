<h1 className="mt-1 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground">Summary</h1>

<Summary>

- Binary search needs sorted data or another monotonic search condition
- Keep the search interval and its boundary convention consistent
- Compare the middle candidate, then exclude it when moving a bound
- Halving the candidate range gives O(log n) time
- An iterative search uses O(1) extra space
- Ordinary search may return any matching index when duplicates exist

</Summary>

---

<h2 className="mt-4 flex items-center gap-2 text-lg font-semibold text-foreground">Common Mistakes</h2>

<Mistake>
Searching unsorted values or assuming that a midpoint comparison can discard a half without a valid ordering.
</Mistake>

<Mistake>
Mixing inclusive and exclusive boundary rules, which can skip candidates or cause an infinite loop.
</Mistake>

<Mistake>
Forgetting to move past the midpoint after it has already been checked.
</Mistake>
