<Callout type="info">
  A typical sliding-window pass is O(n) because each boundary moves forward at most n times. The exact update depends on the state you maintain, such as a sum or a character-frequency map.
</Callout>

<Hint>
For a sum-based variable window, shrinking after the sum reaches a target is straightforward when values are nonnegative: removing a value cannot increase the sum. With negative values, that reasoning may fail.
